✨ About PlayVerse

PlayVerse is a gaming hub prototype where you can play mini-games, build your own games, customize an avatar, collect rewards, and explore a social Plaza.

Version: Ultimate local prototype · Designed to work in a mobile browser, including Android tablets.

🔒 What is local vs online?

✅ Works in this prototype

Playable mini-games, game creation, avatar choices, local friend-request demos, shop purchases, XP, coins, local rankings, and browser saves.

☁️ Needs online setup

Real Google sign-in and cloud saves need a configured authentication service and database. Real friends, live multiplayer, global leaderboards, and public game publishing need a secure backend and hosting.

🏅 About the Verified badge

The badge here is a PlayVerse demo rank. It is not official identity verification, and local progress can be changed on the device. A real rank system should check achievements on a server.

🛡️ Safety reminder

Never share your password, home address, phone number, or other private details in game chats. Only sign in through a trusted Google sign-in screen after the app is configured.
